The Rivero--de Vries electroweak observation is formulated as a two-branch secular problem built from \(J=s(s+1)\), with roots of \(x^2+Jx-J=0\).  The positive-root quotient \(1-x_+(3/4)/x_+(2)=0.22310132\ldots\) is used as a descriptive spectral clue near the W/Z complex-pole weak-angle ratio, with the low-energy pole spectrum taken as the working placement hypothesis.  The negative branch belongs to the same diagonalization and is treated as a candidate scalar/order-parameter datum whose physical meaning requires a gauge-invariant scalar functional.  Endpoint, interval Kaluza--Klein, and localized \(G_2\) settings supply source-theory addresses for a two-channel kernel; the central theorem targets are the ordered assignment \((J_W,J_Z)=(3/4,2)\), the derivation of the DeVries kernel entries, and the pole-matched scalar interpretation of the partner branch.
