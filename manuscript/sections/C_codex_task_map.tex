\section{Codex task map}
\label{app:codex-map}

The agentic workflow used for this manuscript is encoded in `codex\_tasks/'.  The tasks separate algebra, pole-mass scheme, electroweak interpretation, string/Kaluza--Klein/\(G_2\) completion, flavor boundary, global-form appendix, and referee review.  This separation is useful because the numerical clue and the conceptual gap are different objects.  The numerical clue is the combined positive- and negative-branch pattern.  The conceptual gap is the derivation of Eq.~\eqref{eq:Jassignment} and the placement of the secular radical on the low-energy pole spectrum.
