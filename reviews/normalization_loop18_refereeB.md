# Loop 18 Referee B

Role: independent PRD referee focused on string, Kaluza--Klein, \(G_2\), source
control, and manuscript structure.

## Central Claim

O10 proposes a dimensional interpolation with full gauge
\(D=11\to10\to9\), KK \(7\to6\to5\), and colourless electroweak
\(D=7\to6\to5\), KK \(3\to2\to1\).  The \(D=10\) and colourless \(D=6\)
entries must derive the DeVries two-channel kernel, ordered \(J\)-assignment,
electroweak-ray map, and pole matching.

## Strongest Source-Supported Result

Witten 1981 supports the \(D=11,n_{\rm KK}=7\) endpoint in the
isometry-as-gauge-field KK class: the Standard Model gauge algebra requires at
least seven compact dimensions in that setting, and the same construction has
a fermion quantum-number obstruction.  The six-dimensional string citations
support six-dimensional arenas through duality, small-instanton dynamics, and
anomaly constraints.

## Weakest Inference

The \(D=9,n_{\rm KK}=5\) endpoint as \(SU(3)\times U(1)_{\rm em}\) needs
derivation.  Witten gives a five-dimensional \(SU(3)\times U(1)\) symmetry
address; charge normalization, electroweak embedding, and the allowed Higgs-ray
limit remain open.

## Hidden Assumptions

- Witten's seven-dimensional minimum is applied inside a specific KK framework.
- The Witten endpoint and the DeVries electroweak ray are linked by a source
  map.
- The five-dimensional symmetry address represents the formal heavy-vector
  endpoint.
- The \(7/6/5\) count has a six-dimensional middle object with two light
  channels.
- The colourless \(3/2/1\) count treats colour as a spectator while preserving
  charge lattice, anomalies, and global form.
- Six-dimensional string dualities provide arenas; the DeVries kernel remains
  a separate derivation.

## Required Revisions

1. Soften the displayed \(D=9\) endpoint line to \(SU(3)\times U(1)_{\rm geom}\)
   or an explicitly candidate geometric \(U(1)\).
2. State after the source-status table that Witten's five-sphere material
   supplies the symmetry address, while electromagnetic normalization and
   embedding remain target data.
3. Update O10 so the six-dimensional interior requires a chirality and anomaly
   account before promotion.
4. Add page-level source notes for the Witten five-sphere passage and the
   six-dimensional string anchors before stronger manuscript prose.
5. Upgrade the colourless count with a source or derivation for spectator
   colour, charge lattice, anomalies, and global form.
