## Central claim

The string/KK/\(G_2\) program is framed as a kernel-derivation chain:
\[
\mathcal S\to\mathcal H_J=\operatorname{span}\{h_J,a_J\}
\to K_J(\lambda)\to \lambda^2+J\lambda-J
\to (J_W,J_Z)=(3/4,2)\to \sPole .
\]
The manuscript gives source-backed addresses for possible mechanisms and
states the missing proof obligations.

## Strongest source-supported result

The interval Kaluza--Klein route is strongest, since the local source corpus
supports boundary mass and kinetic terms modifying interval eigenvalue
conditions.  The \(G_2\) section is credible as localization language through
singular gauge loci, chiral matter, Higgs-bundle data, flow lines, and
deformation modes.  The DeVries entry matching remains open in both cases.

## Weakest inference

The dimensional interpolation remains the weakest physics inference.  Witten's
seven-extra-dimensional \(SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)\) endpoint and
six-dimensional string vacua are source-backed.  The middle-dimensional lines
\((D,n_{\rm KK})=(10,6)\) and \((D,n_{\rm KK}^{\rm ew})=(6,2)\) remain
Schur-complement reconstruction labels.  The five-dimensional endpoint needs
an electroweak embedding showing that the surviving \(U(1)\) is
\(U(1)_{\rm em}\).
