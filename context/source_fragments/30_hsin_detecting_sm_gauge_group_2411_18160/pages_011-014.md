   Below is the complete list of possible fractional values of ξ, and what choice of gauge
group Γ = Zn ⊂ Z6 and fractionalization class k ∈ k ∈ Z6/n can give rise to that ξ:
  • If ξ ∈ Z + 1/6, then Γ = 1, k = 5.
  • If ξ ∈ Z + 5/6, then Γ = 1, k = 3.
  • If ξ ∈ Z + 1/3, then either Γ = 1, k = 0 or Γ = Z2 , k = 2.
  • If ξ ∈ Z + 2/3, then either Γ = 1, k = 2 or Γ = Z2 , k = 0.
  • If ξ ∈ Z + 1/2, then Γ = 1, k = 1 or Γ = Z3 , k = 1.
  • If ξ ∈ Z, ξ has no fractional part, then Γ = 1, k = 4, or Γ = Z2 , k = 1 or Γ = Z3 , k = 0,
    or Γ = Z6 .
