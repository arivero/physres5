### Refutations / corrections
1. **(MINOR — overreach in (a): "D†D = j(j+1)" presented as automatic.)** A Spin^c Dirac operator's
   square is, by Parthasarathy/Lichnerowicz, C_G − C_K + |ρ|² (Casimir minus isotropy-spinor Casimir
   plus a curvature constant), NOT the bare SU(2)_L Casimir j(j+1) with unit coefficient and zero
   shift. Equating D_patch†D_patch to exactly j(j+1) requires the shift/curvature terms to vanish and
   the normalization to be unity — an identification of the *same logical status as c=1*. T5 part (a)
   states it as definitional and part (c) calls "a first-order D_patch whose square is the Casimir"
   geometrically automatic; the docs are more careful ("Find D_patch such that D†D = j(j+1)" —
   Deliverable). Correct framing: the Casimir *values* (2, 3/4) are forced; that some D's square
   equals them with no shift and unit coeff is an extra assumption. Does not change any number or the
   (c) "extra-postulate" verdict; it *strengthens* it (there are now TWO normalization assumptions,
   D†D=j(j+1) and c=1, not one).
2. **(MINOR — §3.6 "structural overlap" is loose.)** As above: the Lichnerowicz square ≠ the
   diagonal-(−D†D) De Vries block. The (b) verdict "INDEPENDENT (mixed)" is nonetheless correct
   because it rests on c=1 being absent from every public construction, which is true.
3. **(MINOR — convention assumption in transpose-equivalence.)** The two A-conventions share a
   spectrum only if D is normal (DD†=D†D=J) on the relevant mode. True for an isospectral Dirac-type
   map but stated as if unconditional. Harmless for the result.
4. **(NONE — assignment selection.)** Beyond "extra-postulate c=1," even *selecting* the specific
   pair (j=1, j=1/2) as the W/Z sectors among the available q8-odd doublets (q8=±1 AND ±3) and the
   integer-j towers is an assignment, not forced by the parity law alone. T5 already lists "the
   assignment (j=1/2 ↔ doublet, j=1 ↔ adjoint)" as not derived from the metric, so this is
   acknowledged, not a new refutation. Flagged for completeness.
