# Loop 16 Referee A: Electroweak and Scheme Discipline

## Central Claim

O17 belongs in the paper as a conditional theorem target.  The symbolic chain is
\[
\text{DeVries kernel}
+\text{negative-branch vacuum map}
+\text{electroweak embedding}
\Longrightarrow
g^2=\frac{2x_+(3/4)}{|x_-(2)|},
\qquad
\alpha=\frac{g^2\sin^2\theta_{dV}}{4\pi}.
\]
Its status is conjectural until the scalar theorem derives
\(v=\sqrt2 M_-(2)\).

## Strongest Source-Supported Result

The low-energy electroweak identities
\[
M_W^2=\frac{g^2v^2}{4},\quad
M_Z^2=\frac{(g^2+g'^2)v^2}{4},\quad
e=g\sin\theta_W,\quad
\alpha=\frac{e^2}{4\pi}
\]
are the correct bridge for a future alpha target.  The existing pole-scheme
section and scalar caveat already supply the right manuscript infrastructure.

## Weakest Inference

The scale placement near a hadronic or confinement-scale alpha value lacks a
defined observable, scheme, threshold prescription, and source-backed coupling.
The manuscript must choose among on-shell \(\alpha(0)\), \(\MSbar\)
\(\hat\alpha(\mu)\), effective \(\alpha(q^2)\), Euclidean
hadronic-vacuum-polarization conventions, or a threshold-matched EFT coupling.

## Hidden Assumptions

- \(v=\sqrt2M_-(2)\) selects the \(J=2\) negative branch as the Higgs radial datum.
- The branch \(v\) and the Standard Model \(v\) live in one scheme.
- The \(W\)-branch normalization and scalar-branch normalization share one \(\mu\).
- The tree relation \(e=g\sin\theta_W\) can be joined to a pole-spectrum angle through a matching remainder.
- The \(D=9\) endpoint \(U(1)\) is \(U(1)_{\rm em}\).
- Compactification fixes the absolute gauge kinetic normalization and the charge normalization.
- Hadronic running and thresholds can be included without adding an independent input parameter.
