```text

```

has priority over the seven-sphere analogy.  It keeps the visible bookkeeping separated:

```text

S^2              weak or projective-axis data,
S^1 in S^5       candidate EM or hypercharge period after normalization.
```

The product-to-twisted-fibre audit in
`notes/D5D7_Product_Twisted_Fibre_Map_Audit.md` adds the missing endpoint map test.  The product
has fibre `S^1_H x S^2_W` over `CP^2`, while the Aloff-Wallach fibre contains
`SO(2)->SO(3)->S^2` with projective Hopf twist.  The product remains the preferred diagnostic;
the twist class has to be tracked before any D7 comparison changes `P_B,A_B,m_B,gamma_B`.

```text

```

remains useful for teaching restored nonabelian fibre intuition and for comparison with a highly
symmetric carrier.  It tends to hide the `CP^2`, Hopf/lens, and weak-axis factors that the
Standard Model checks need to see separately.

The active D7 carrier still comes from the Aloff-Wallach `SO(3)` bundle over `CP^2`.  The product
`S^5 x S^2` serves as endpoint diagnostic, comparison space, possible limit, quotient target, or
lecture model until a source calculation connects it to the active branch.
