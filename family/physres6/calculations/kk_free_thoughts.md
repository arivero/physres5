# Free exploration: the Kaluza-Klein structure hinted by DeVries

## 1. The half-integer entry fingerprints S³ = SU(2), not S²  [firm]

`J=3/4` means `s=1/2`, a half-integer SU(2) representation. Functions on
`S² = SO(3)/SO(2)` carry only integer `l` (`J=l(l+1)=0,2,6,…`). Half-integer
harmonics are single-valued only on the double cover `S³=SU(2)`, where
Peter–Weyl gives `L²(SU(2)) = ⊕_j V_j⊗V_j*`, `j=0,½,1,…` — all spins, including
`s=½`. So the W slot (`J=3/4`) cannot live on `S²`; it requires the group
manifold `S³=SU(2)` (or any internal space carrying its spinor harmonics). The
half-integer DeVries entry is a direct fingerprint of `S³`. This matches the
operator form (Henkel–Lauret Lemma 2.1 on SU(2)) found earlier.

## 2. Q(J) as a flux-compactification mass matrix  [promising]

In Freund–Rubin `AdS₄ × M` vacua, background flux mixes modes of different
form-degree at each KK level; the linearized mass matrix is a `2×2` with a flux
off-diagonal and a Laplacian diagonal, and its eigenvalues can be
BF-admissibly negative. `Q(J)=[[0,√J],[√J,−J]]` reads as exactly this: Laplacian
diagonal (`−J`, AdS/flux sign), flux/derivative off-diagonal (`√J`). Then
`tr=det=−J` is a tuning of flux to curvature — a critical/self-dual flux
condition, not an arbitrary ansatz. The negative branch is the BF-mode whose
condensation shifts the radius. This gives the `−J` a physical home (flux +
curvature) and the trace=det condition a meaning.

## 3. Electroweak breaking as old-string tachyon condensation  [speculative]

The 1968–71 dual model / bosonic string carried a tachyon in its ground state.
The DeVries negative branch is tachyonic and condenses to give the vacuum. Read
literally: the electroweak vacuum is a tachyon condensate of the hadronic-scale
(1971) string, and the negative branch is that tachyon (Sen-style condensation =
a shift to a new vacuum). This ties together the IR/hadronic string
(`α' ~ (1 GeV)⁻²`), its tachyon, and the order parameter. The coupling, a
worldsheet/IR quantity, is then read at the string scale ~1 GeV — which is where
`1/α=135.3` sits.

## 4. Higgs = radion  [promising, grounded]

The order parameter that fixes `R` is, in the cleanest reading, the radion. So
the Higgs IS the size modulus and `⟨H⟩` IS the stabilized radius. One field, two
roles — the sharpest form of "breaking = moduli stabilization" (Bucci; Haba–Oda).

## 5. W/Z assignment from the internal isometry  [speculative — handle on O1]

On `S³=SU(2)` (squashed to `SU(2)×U(1)`), gauge bosons come from isometries.
Candidate: the charged W ↔ the off-diagonal (raising/lowering) isometry
directions, carrying the half-integer doublet label (`J=3/4`); the neutral Z ↔
the Cartan/adjoint (integer, `J=2`). Doublet (off-diagonal) vs adjoint (Cartan)
as the origin of `(3/4,2)`.

## 6. Two scales  [speculative]

`g²=x₊(3/4)x₊(2)` gives `μ² = v M_Z/2`, so `μ ≈ √(v M_Z/2) ≈ 107 GeV` is the
geometric mean of `v` and `M_Z` — the intercept/KK scale. The coupling lives at
the string slope `1/√α' ~ 1 GeV`. The ~100× gap is the separation between the
KK/intercept scale (spectrum) and the worldsheet/coupling scale (IR). In the old
string the coupling is a worldsheet quantity, so `α` sits at ~1 GeV while the
spectrum sits at ~100 GeV.

## 7. Branch inversion as s–t duality  [speculative]

`x→−J/x` exchanging the two branches echoes Veneziano s–t channel duality (the
amplitude symmetric under `s↔t`). The `+`/`−` branches as the two channels;
the self-duality `tr=det` as the channel-symmetry condition.
