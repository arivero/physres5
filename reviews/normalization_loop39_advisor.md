# Loop 39 Advisor

## Conceptual Route

Use a graded-curvature projection route.  Treat Coquereaux's odd generators
\(\Omega_\pm,\Omega'_\pm\) as finite electroweak Dirac data, with the even
anticommutators supplying \(I_3,Y,Q\).  Project the generalized curvature norm
onto
\[
\mathcal H_J^{\rm sc}=\operatorname{span}\{h_J,a_J^{\gamma^\perp}\}.
\]
The audit tests whether the scalar-vector entry and the diagonal \(J\) entry
come from the same projected inner product.

## Mechanism To Test

Test a CHM interval with an \(SU(2|1)\)-type boundary superconnection at one
endpoint.  Bulk \(SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y\) gauge fields supply
\(K_T^{\rm DtN}\).  The boundary superconnection supplies
\(K^{\rm brane}_{\rm sc}\), scalar \(A_5/\pi_i\) data, and the finite odd/even
projection.

## Source To Read

Acquire Coquereaux--Esposito-Farèse--Scheck, "An \(SU(2|1)\) theory of
electroweak interactions described by algebraic superconnections," Int. J.
Mod. Phys. A 7 (1992) 6555--6593.  The Strasbourg talk points to this paper for
detailed electroweak Lagrangian and bosonic-sector calculations.
