## New Conceptual Route

Conjecture: O17 defines a coupling from a branch-normalized vacuum.  The
positive branch supplies the projective weak angle.  The \(J=2\) negative branch
is assigned to the radial Higgs/vacuum datum:
\[
v=\sqrt2\,\mu\sqrt{|x_-(2)|}.
\]
Then the electroweak identity \(M_W=gv/2\) gives
\[
g^2=\frac{4M_W^2}{v^2}
=\frac{2x_+(3/4)}{|x_-(2)|},
\qquad
\alpha_\star=\frac{g^2\sin^2\theta_{dV}}{4\pi}.
\]
This turns the negative-branch scalar issue into a coupling-normalization
theorem target.
