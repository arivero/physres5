# Weak point 1 — The 2x2 seed matrix is an engineered ansatz, not a derived structure

- (a) The specific form `M_R^2 = m_0^2 * [[0, sqrt(C2(R))], [sqrt(C2(R)), -C2(R)]]` is asserted, not deduced. There is no symmetry, gauge invariance, or representation-theoretic argument that forces the (1,1) entry to vanish, the (2,2) entry to equal `-C2(R)`, or the off-diagonal entry to equal `+sqrt(C2(R))`. The Casimir appears at three positions with three different powers (0, 1/2, 1) tuned to produce one specific eigenvalue ratio.
- (b) Replacing the (1,1) zero with `epsilon * m_0^2` for any `epsilon = O(0.05)` destroys the eigenvalue ratio that gives `M_W/m_0` and `m_h,bare/m_0`. Equivalently, rescaling the off-diagonal entry by `(1 + eta)` for `eta ~ 0.02` breaks the fit to `M_W` at the percent level. The model has zero structural robustness against the most natural perturbations of its own ansatz.
- (c) A referee would call this numerology: the matrix has been reverse-engineered so that its characteristic polynomial `lambda^2 + C2 lambda - C2 = 0` produces the desired root ratio, then dressed in Casimir notation to look group-theoretic. The honest alternative is to write `M_R^2 = m_0^2 * [[a, b], [b, c]]` and observe that three free parameters per representation have been hidden as `(0, sqrt(C2), -C2)`.

# Weak point 2 — `M_W` is not predicted; it is `sin^2 theta_W` re-expressed via a Casimir ratio

- (a) `M_Z` and `G_F` are declared experimental inputs. The seed scale `m_0 ~ 106.58 GeV` is fixed by requiring the triplet upper eigenvalue to equal `M_W^2`, after which `M_Z` enters only through `v` and `G_F`. The "prediction" `M_W = 80.369 GeV` is therefore a one-parameter fit (`m_0` tuned to `M_W`), not a derivation of the weak mixing angle from group theory.
- (b) Treating `M_W` (not `m_0`) as the anchor — which the project's own Phase 1 notes admit is required under "Fit B" — exposes the circularity: any monotonic function of `C2(3)` and `C2(2)` whose ratio happens to lie near `(M_W/m_h,bare)^2` would reproduce the fit. The smallest invalidating modification is to demand a genuine prediction of `rho = M_W^2/(M_Z^2 cos^2 theta_W) = 1` from the Casimir structure; the model does not deliver this and instead consumes `M_Z` as input.
- (c) A referee would point out that the SM already predicts `M_W` from `(M_Z, G_F, alpha)` at sub-permille accuracy via electroweak radiative corrections, and the Casimir-locked scheme adds no new content beyond reparametrizing one experimental ratio. The natural alternative reading is: "the model fits one number with one parameter and presents this as a two-Casimir success."

# Weak point 3 — Delta is an undetermined free parameter that does the final fit to `m_h`

- (a) SEED.md explicitly lists "the algebraic origin of Delta" under NOT YET DERIVED. The shift `Delta ~ 700 GeV^2` is exactly the value needed to drag `m_h,bare = 122.4 GeV` onto `m_h,phys = 125.3 GeV`. No symmetry forbids `Delta = 0`, no first-principles calculation fixes its magnitude, and the descriptor "trace-preserving spurion" is already flagged in the project memory as a misnomer (the actual perturbation is rank-1 along the eigenvector `v_-`).
- (b) Removing Delta (`Delta -> 0`) collapses the Higgs prediction by `~3 GeV`, a `~25 sigma` discrepancy against PDG. Conversely, any model with one tunable additive correction to one eigenvalue can hit any single mass to arbitrary precision; the Higgs "prediction" therefore carries zero predictive content beyond what is put in by hand.
- (c) A referee would label this a counterterm masquerading as structure. The natural alternative is the SM interpretation: `m_h^2 = 2 lambda v^2` with `lambda` an independent renormalized coupling — exactly one free parameter, exactly one mass fitted, no Casimir scaffolding required. The Casimir-locked seesaw replaces `lambda` with `(m_0, Delta)` and gains nothing.
