# Analytical task: negative branch and Higgs/order-parameter scale

## Algebra

The roots obey

\[
x_+(J)+x_-(J)=-J,
\qquad
x_+(J)x_-(J)=-J.
\]

Thus the negative branch is fixed once the positive branch and \(J\) are chosen.

## Candidate interpretations

1. Tachyonic Higgs mass parameter in the unbroken Lagrangian.
2. Auxiliary mass-squared branch whose determinant enforces the positive vector branch.
3. Brane/orbit partner branch in a two-channel secular equation.
4. Regge-intercept correction that preserves high-spin tension.

## Required derivation

A valid manuscript claim must exhibit a gauge-invariant map from \(x_-(J)\) to at least one of

\[
\mu_H^2,
\quad \lambda,
\quad v^2,
\quad m_h^2,
\quad \Pi_{VV}(s),
\quad \text{or a compactification eigenvalue}.
\]

## Falsifiable checks

- Does the predicted scale agree with the Higgs pole mass within a stated scheme?
- Does the branch produce the correct sign for EWSB?
- Does it remain stable under scheme changes?
