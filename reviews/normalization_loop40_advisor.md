# Loop 40 Advisor

## Conceptual Route

Use a boundary electroweak superconnection on a CHM interval.  Coquereaux's
finite \(SU(2|1)\) gauge-Higgs complex supplies the boundary light space, and
the CHM Dirichlet-to-Neumann kernel supplies the transverse pole operator.
This merges O1, O3, O4, O8, O10, and O20 into one source package with one
inner product, photon projection, \(J\)-normalization, scalar functional, and
pole remainder.

## Mechanism To Test

Use a five-dimensional interval gauge theory with brane-localized generalized
connection
\[
\mathcal A_{\rm bdry}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
A_{\rm even}&\Phi_{\rm odd}\\
\Phi_{\rm odd}^\dagger&A'_{\rm even}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The even current channel comes from \(A_\mu^T\).  The odd
Higgs/order-parameter channel comes from \((A_5,\pi,\Phi_{\rm odd})\).  The
bulk is integrated by a Dirichlet-to-Neumann map, the photon zero mode is
subtracted, and the projected kernel is tested against the DeVries block.

\(Z/P\) rotation, and curvature terms support a source-normalized projection.
