C   Boundary Conditions

In this appendix we provide some details underpinning the computation of the D-cohomology
groups in section 4.1. The argument was originally introduced by Witten [39] for closed
manifolds. The extension to the manifolds with boundary includes the additional subtlety
of choosing the appropriate boundary conditions, which allow us to apply the Hodge theory
arguments on manifolds with boundary.
    Following [45] we consider the case where we have a single boundary component. We first
focus on defining the appropriate domains of the standard operators d and d† and then show
