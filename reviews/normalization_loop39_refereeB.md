# Loop 39 Referee B

## Central Claim

Coquereaux supplies electroweak superconnection data relevant to O1/O20:
grading, Higgs-as-connection language, finite matrix representations, curvature
construction, and Standard Model gauge-Higgs architecture.  These data define an
arena for a projection theorem.

## Source Facts

The source explicitly supplies scalar zero-forms and gauge one-forms in a
graded matrix connection, a \(\mathbb Z_2\)-graded associative algebra, a
generalized curvature \(\mathcal F=d\mathcal A+\mathcal A\odot\mathcal A\), and
an \(SU(2|1)\) construction whose even gauge algebra is \(SU(2)\times U(1)\).
It also supplies source-internal weak-angle normalizations, including the
extended-family \(3/8\) value.

## Unproved Inference

The identification of one Coquereaux finite basis with the full DeVries basis
carrying \(P_W\), \(P_Z\), \(P_\gamma\), \(B_J\), \(u_-\), and
\(\Delta_{\rm sc}\) remains open.  The source gives electroweak grading and
finite matrices; the DeVries quotient and two-channel operator require an
extraction theorem.

## Hidden Assumptions

The odd Higgs channel can be projected to \(J_H=3/4\).  The photon-orthogonal
even current can be projected to \(J_{\rm adj}=2\).  These projections share one
inner product and one dimensionless normalization.  The Coquereaux source angle
and the DeVries pole quotient can be related by a stated matching remainder.
String, Kaluza-Klein, interval, or \(G_2\) mechanisms inherit the finite basis
after extra-channel decoupling.
