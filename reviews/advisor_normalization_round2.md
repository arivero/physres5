# Advisor normalization round 2

## One new conceptual route

Treat O10 as a dimensional Schur-complement problem.  The \(D=10\)
full-gauge interior, or the colourless \(D=6\) interior, is a light
two-channel boundary sector obtained after integrating out an endpoint,
bulk, or compactification tower.  This ties O10 directly to O4 through
\(K_J^{\rm eff}(\lambda)\), to O1 through
\(\mathcal H_J=\mathrm{span}\{h_J,a_J\}\), and to O8 through the required
matching from the effective kernel to \(\Delta^{-1}_{V,T}(s_{W,Z})\).

## One string/Kaluza--Klein mechanism to test

bulk-gauge boundary value can produce
\[
\Sigma_{hh}=0,\qquad
\Sigma_{aa}=J,\qquad
\Sigma_{ha}\Sigma_{ah}=J,

while preserving the electroweak ray and the photon zero mode.
