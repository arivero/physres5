## central claim

O18 and O19 frame the higher DeVries branches as conditional string/brane
theorem targets: \(j\) is a sector label, \(N_{\rm osc}\) supplies ordinary
Regge/KK tower growth, and the roots may encode a bounded positive sector plus
a linearly growing negative-sector diagnostic.

obligations.  Tong and Polchinski support open-string Regge towers,
Chan--Paton matrix data, D-brane gauge fields, adjoint scalars, and T-duality
vocabulary.  Target VIII separates \(j\), \(N_{\rm osc}\), and physical spin.
Target IX records the algebraic facts \(x_+x_-=-J\),
\(x_+(s(s+1))=1+O(s^{-2})\), and \(|x_-|\sim s(s+1)\).

## weakest inference

The O19 jump from branch asymptotics to D0/space-filling brane duality is the
fragile step.  The rotating-brane exponent source, negative-branch mass
reading, and boundary-condition duality map remain absent.  O18's \(j=3/2\)
slot is an algebraic intercept pending projection, gauge representation,
width, production, decay, and collider ledgers.
