# Project memory for Codex

## Established project facts

- The current manuscript draft was prepared as a REVTeX/Phys Rev C style article aiming at roughly thirty pages. This agentic repository extends it toward a complete manuscript of up to fifty pages.
- The central construction is the DeVries/Rivero Casimir mass operator and its positive-root ratio for \(J=3/4\) and \(J=2\).
- The numerical target is the pole observable \(1-M_W^2/M_Z^2\), not a running weak mixing angle.
- The vacuum scale \(v\) is radial. The DeVries number is treated as angular/projective.
- The construction must preserve the full electroweak broken-to-unbroken ray. Do not introduce independent limits that leave only one of the W or Z massless.
- The W/Z assignment remains a central conceptual calculation. A plausible route uses the Higgs doublet and adjoint/current representation data rather than the observed vector spin.
- The negative root is analytically important. Its relation to the Higgs/order-parameter scale remains open.
- Casimir language is a clue and construction. The project focus is electroweak, still related to Kaluza-Klein, strings, branes, and G2 singularities.
- Generation structure is not to be claimed as direct compactification topology. The working flavor caveat is an SO(32)-flavor interpretation that may or may not be compatible with topology.
