# Normalization loop 13 - Referee B

## Central claim

The manuscript presents endpoint, interval/Kaluza--Klein, and \(G_2\)
mechanisms as candidate source theories for one reduced two-channel kernel.
The positive branch is assigned to the W/Z pole ratio, and the negative branch
is assigned to scalar/order-parameter data after a derived scalar map.

## Strongest source-supported result

The mechanism inventory is strongest.  The interval section uses
source-backed boundary terms as a route to eigenvalue-dependent secular
determinants.  The \(G_2\) section gives a credible local dictionary with
singular loci, localized matter/order-parameter data, deformation modes, and
Higgs-bundle variables.  Both route dictionaries leave the DeVries entries as
theorem targets.

## Weakest inference

The dimensional interpolation parameter is the weakest inference.  Target VI
assumes that the electroweak order parameter supplies one interpolation
parameter represented by compact, boundary, brane, or singular data.  The
sources support KK and string dimensional settings.  The shared control
parameter connecting the Higgs radial ray, dimensional endpoints, the DeVries
kernel, and pole matching remains open.

## Hidden assumptions

- The same reduced basis that yields the DeVries kernel also carries the
  electroweak projectors \(P_W\) and \(P_Z\).
- \(J\) can be read as a Casimir/current datum, overlap product, or
  compactification charge with one normalization.
- A local \(G_2\) determinant survives anomaly, representation, and Standard
  Model global-form tests.
- Witten's five-dimensional symmetry address can be promoted to an endpoint
  with surviving \(U(1)_{\rm em}\) after an electroweak embedding theorem.
- The pole self-energy chain preserves the clean determinant.
