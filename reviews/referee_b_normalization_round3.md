# Referee B normalization round 3

## Central claim

The manuscript assigns the DeVries positive-branch quotient
\(x_+(3/4)/x_+(2)\) to the low-energy W/Z complex-pole mass ratio, with
\(J=3/4\) read from the Higgs doublet/order-parameter channel and \(J=2\)
from the \(SU(2)_L\) adjoint/current channel.

## Strongest source-supported result

The pole-scheme separation is well motivated. Martin--Robertson support the
complex-pole definition \(s_{\rm pole}=M^2-i\Gamma M\) and the warning that
commonly quoted W/Z masses are variable-width Breit--Wigner masses, related by
\(M_{V,\rm BW}^2=M_{V,\rm pole}^2+\Gamma_{V,\rm pole}^2\). The electroweak
mass-matrix discussion in Sec. IV supports the projective W/Z ratio along the
broken-to-unbroken ray.

## Weakest inference

O8 remains the weakest point. The manuscript states the pole placement as the
working reading, while the route sections supply only a matching-chain target.
No source-backed derivation yet carries a string/brane/KK/\(G_2\) kernel
through \(\Gamma_{\rm eff}^{(4)}[H,W,B]\) into the dressed transverse W/Z pole
condition. O2a is stronger, although it still needs convention conditions
stated explicitly.

## Hidden assumptions

The same two-channel kernel controls both the vector pole quotient and the
negative scalar branch. The Breit--Wigner conversion is used in the
\(s_{\rm pole}=M^2-i\Gamma M\) parameterization, while Martin--Robertson also
note a later \((M-i\Gamma/2)^2\) parameterization. W and Z inputs are assumed
to share compatible line-shape conventions before forming \(\sPole\). The
ordered map \((J_W,J_Z)=(3/4,2)\) is assumed after identifying doublet and
adjoint invariants. Radiative corrections are assumed to preserve a meaningful
projective pole-ratio target after matching.
