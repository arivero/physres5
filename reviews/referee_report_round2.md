# Referee report round 2

## Central claim

The manuscript proposes that the DeVries/Rivero two-branch secular equation is a pole-spectrum clue for the electroweak W/Z ratio, with the negative branch pointing toward the scalar/order-parameter side.  The physical burden is to derive the same determinant from an electroweak string/Kaluza--Klein boundary mechanism.

## Strengths

- The draft now states the radical-placement problem clearly.
- The string/Kaluza--Klein material has specific mechanisms: endpoint data, interval boundary spectra, momentum/winding analogy, and localized \(G_2\) operators.
- The electroweak representation correction is visible: \(J=3/4\) belongs to the Higgs/order-parameter side and \(J=2\) to the adjoint/current side.
- The global-form and flavor sections now act as constraints on completions.
- Open derivation gaps are explicit.

## Main criticisms

- The pole-scheme section remains too short for PRD review. It needs formal definitions of pole mass, on-shell/Breit-Wigner conventions, and running weak-angle schemes.
- The endpoint route is still a template. It needs a toy action with named fields and a precise route to \(\kappa_J^2=J\), \(\tau_J=J\).
- The ordered sampling rule remains the largest weakness. The manuscript needs a theorem-shaped statement explaining exactly what would count as a derivation.
- The negative branch section now has targets, but the gauge-invariant scalar map remains open.
- The source matrix needs page-level audit for every historical and string-theory claim.

## Required next revisions

- Expand Sec. III into a formal pole-scheme chapter.
- Expand Sec. IV into a theorem-style electroweak assignment problem.
- Choose one mechanism and write an explicit two-channel model.
- Add an appendix collecting open theorem statements and exact assumptions.
