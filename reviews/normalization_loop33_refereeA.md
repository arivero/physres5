Role: skeptical PRD referee focused on electroweak correctness, scheme
discipline, and overclaiming.

## Strongest source-supported result

The standard gauge-Higgs mass map is secure:
\[
M_W^2=g^2v^2/4,\qquad
M_Z^2=(g^2+g'^2)v^2/4,\qquad
M_\gamma=0.
\]
The Higgs doublet carries \(C_2=3/4\), the \(SU(2)_L\) adjoint current
carries \(C_2=2\), and the pole/Breit-Wigner/\(\MSbar\) distinctions are
isolated in the manuscript.  These data support a theorem target.

## Weakest inference

The ordered map remains the weak point.  Standard electroweak theory gives
charged and neutral vector states from adjoint gauge fields plus hypercharge
mixing.  The manuscript still owes a source rule that makes the charged pole
functional sample the doublet/order-parameter invariant and the neutral massive
pole functional sample the adjoint/current invariant.
