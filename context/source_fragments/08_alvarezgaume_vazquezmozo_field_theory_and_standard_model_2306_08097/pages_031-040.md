this case the electromagnetic field) is in fact a Fock space, i.e., the direct sum of Hilbert spaces spanned

Introduction (see page 7), where while the asymptotic states are described by hadrons, the fundamental
interactions taking place are described in terms of weakly coupled quarks and gluons5 .
