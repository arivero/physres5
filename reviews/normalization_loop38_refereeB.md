# Loop 38 Referee B

## Central Claim

O20 asks whether
\[
Q_{{\rm red},J}=Q_{{\rm dR},J}+B_J,\qquad
Q_{{\rm dR},J}=\begin{pmatrix}0&\sqrt J\\ \sqrt J&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B_J=\operatorname{diag}(0,-J)
\]
can arise from a finite broken \(N=2\) supersymmetric quantum-mechanics
reduction.  The same two-channel basis must also carry \(P_W\), \(P_Z\), the
negative-branch scalar map, and the pole matching chain.

## Strongest Source-Supported Result

The off-diagonal square-root target is source-supported.  Witten supplies the
Hodge/SUSY-QM template, and a normalized eigenpair can yield
\[
D_Je^0_J=\sqrt J\,e^1_J,\qquad
D_J^\dagger e^1_J=\sqrt J\,e^0_J .
\]
Coquereaux supplies electroweak superconnection vocabulary; Fayet supplies
massive gauge-multiplet partner vocabulary; Gates--Rana supplies worldline and
spin supersymmetry vocabulary.

## Weakest Inference

The diagonal term \(B_J=\operatorname{diag}(0,-J)\) carries the main burden.
The manuscript needs a stated action, boundary condition, endpoint algebra,
localized \(G_2\) operator, or SQM breaking term that derives this entry in the
same basis as the Hodge factorization.  The \(SU(2|1)\) odd electroweak grading
also needs a genuine supercharge construction if it is used as part of
physical \(N=2\) supersymmetry.

## Hidden Assumptions

The route assumes one inner product, one finite two-state projection, absence
of extra light channels, exact normalization \(J=s(s+1)=\lambda_J\), a
dimensionless \(B_J\) after factoring \(\mu^2\), a stable sign convention, a
photon-compatible projection, and a pole-scheme map from the reduced kernel to
\(M_{W,\rm pole}^2/M_{Z,\rm pole}^2\).
