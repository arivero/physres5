## Conceptual route

Make O5 a rank-and-ray theorem inside the Target 0 framework.  Define the
electroweak deformation as a one-parameter family with fixed projective
coupling direction, persistent photon null vector, and simultaneous W/Z mass
collapse:
\[
\mathcal M_{\rm EW}^2(t)=
\rho(t)\,
\begin{pmatrix}
g^2 P_\pm & 0\\
0 &
\begin{pmatrix}
g^2 & -gg'\\
-gg' & g'^2
\end{pmatrix}
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\rho(t)=\frac{v(t)^2}{4}.
\]
Any Target 0 source family must preserve this rank pattern and the ordered
projectors \(P_W\) and \(P_Z\).

\[
S_{\rm bdry}\supset \delta(y-y_0)(D_\mu H)^\dagger D^\mu H .
\]
Integrate out the bulk tower by Schur complement and test whether the same
boundary Higgs parameter controls charged and neutral sectors while leaving
the photon null mode exact.
