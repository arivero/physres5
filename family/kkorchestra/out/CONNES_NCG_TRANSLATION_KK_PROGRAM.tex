\begin{abstract}
This manuscript translates the current \(\CP^2\)/Aloff--Wallach
Kaluza--Klein programme into Connes-style noncommutative geometry.  The
translation uses spectral triples, real structures, inner fluctuations,
boundary triples, finite wall modules, and spectral-action language to
separate five layers: compact geometry, endpoint diagnostics, the D6
branch interface, the orthodox \(\mthree\) custodial Higgs block, and the
open retained-response source problem.  The main conclusion is deliberately
conservative.  The Connes vocabulary clarifies the missing object: a
source-derived quadratic form on the retained anti-graph mismatch.  It also
keeps the established electroweak result \(\rho=1\) in the single
\(\mthree\) branch separate from any future De Vries-style spectral
refinement.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematical Expansion Convention}

The project uses compact dictionaries and crosswalks, but a row gets
derivational use only after an expansion supplies variables, modules,
operator or action, domain, first and second variation or spectral readout,
normalization, residuals, and status label.  Claim-only prose can orient,
compare, reject a shortcut, or name a target; it cannot supply a coefficient,
period, multiplier, or equality.  The reusable rule is recorded in
\cite{math-expansion}, and corpus coverage is recorded in
\cite{math-expansion-coverage}.  The package atlas in
\cite{ncg-package-atlas} records the algebra, Hilbert data, operator or
action, trace, residual status, and anchors for the major Connes homes.  Each
compact statement is read as expanded here, expanded in a named file, cited
source, conditional target, filter, obstruction, or rejected shortcut before
it can enter a derivation.
In this manuscript, a source statement that lacks the variables, modules,
maps, domain, variation or spectral readout, normalization, residuals, and use
status is demoted to orientation or control text.  It supplies no source
coefficient, endpoint period, sector multiplier, equality, or prediction.
The present manuscript uses the following templates.

\subsection{Retained Mismatch Source}

The retained boundary source uses the orientation
\[
  P_B:F_B\longrightarrow E_B,\qquad
  h\in E_B,\quad p\in F_B,
\]
where \(h\) is the anti-graph mismatch and \(p\) is the retained response.
A positive mismatch source expands as
\[
  S_{\mathrm{mis}}^{(2)}(h,p)
  =\frac12
    \langle h-P_Bp,\ A_B(h-P_Bp)\rangle_{E_B}.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\begin{aligned}
  S_{\mathrm{mis}}^{(2)}(h,p)
   ={}&\frac12\langle h,A_Bh\rangle_{E_B}
       -\operatorname{Re}\langle h,A_BP_Bp\rangle_{E_B}  \\
     &+\frac12
       \langle p,P_B^\dagger A_BP_Bp\rangle_{F_B}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The response-module kernel before canonical normalization is
\[
  K_B=P_B^\dagger A_BP_B .
\]
If
\[
  K_B\vert_r=\gamma_B^2\mu_r\Id+\sigma_r,
  \qquad
  \|p_r\|_{\mathrm{kin}}^2=m_B\|p_r\|_{F_B}^2,
\]
then the canonical sector readout is
\[
  K_r^{\mathrm{can}}
    =m_B^{-1}K_B\vert_r
    =\frac{\gamma_B^2}{m_B}\mu_r\Id
      +m_B^{-1}\sigma_r .
\]
For scalar softness \(A_B\vert_r=w_B\Id+\Delta_r^A\), the common quotient is
\[
  q_B=\frac{w_B\gamma_B^2}{m_B}.
\]
The multiplier \(\mu_r\) stays neutral until \(P_B^\dagger A_BP_B\) has
been computed from the source package.

\subsection{Schur, Boundary, and Determinant Expansions}

A local or finite auxiliary source uses variables \(z\) and \(h\):
\[
  S^{(2)}(h,z)
    =\frac12\langle z,Kz\rangle
     +\operatorname{Re}\langle z,JR h\rangle
     +\frac12\langle Rh,ERh\rangle .
\]
Eliminating \(z\) gives
\[
  A_{\mathrm{eff}}
    =R^\dagger(E-J^\dagger K^{-1}J)R.
\]
The finite scale belongs to \(K,J,E,R\) and to the trace used on the local
module.

A boundary or singular carrier records
\[
  D_{\min},\quad D_{\max},\quad
  \Gamma_0,\quad \Gamma_1,
\]
and the Green form
\[
  \omega(\phi,\psi)
  =\langle\Gamma_1\phi,\Gamma_0\psi\rangle
   -\langle\Gamma_0\phi,\Gamma_1\psi\rangle .
\]
The Weyl or Calderon operator, extension relation, and finite-part trace
provide domain and spectral data.  Source status begins when those data
compute \(A_B\) or \(K_B=P_B^\dagger A_BP_B\) on the retained modules.

A determinant route starts with an \(h\)-dependent family
\[
  L_B(h)=L_0+V_1[h]+\frac12V_2[h,h]+\cdots,\qquad G_0=L_0^{-1}.
\]
The second variation has the schematic form
\[
  \delta_h^2\log\det L_B
   =\Tr(G_0V_2[\cdot,\cdot])
    -\Tr(G_0V_1[\cdot]G_0V_1[\cdot])
    +\delta_h^2 S_{\mathrm{ct}} .
\]
The sign, subtraction, zero-mode treatment, counterterm, and trace are part
of the source data.

\subsection{Endpoint, Gravity, and Tower Expansions}

Endpoint rows record representation and period data:
\[
  \rho_{\mathrm{end}}(Q_{\mathrm{EM}}),\qquad
  Q_{\mathrm{EM}}H_0=0,\qquad
  \ker(\Phi_{\mathrm{period}}),\qquad
  \chi:\mathbb Z_n\to \U(1).
\]
Gauge kinetic coefficients require normalized generators:
\[
  f_X=\Tr_F(\rho(T_X)^2)\,\mathcal I_X .
\]
The weak-angle ratio has prediction status only after the weak and
hypercharge coefficients use the same normalization,
\[
  \sin^2\theta_W=\frac{f_2}{f_2+f_Y}.
\]

The higher-dimensional metric expansion has the form
\[
  g_D
  =g_4
   +G_{ab}(y,u)
    (dy^a+K_i^{\,a}A^i)(dy^b+K_j^{\,b}A^j),
\]
and reduction of the Einstein--Hilbert or spectral action gives
\[
  S\mapsto
  M_4^2R_4
  +f_{ij}(u)F^iF^j
  +G_{AB}(u)\partial u^A\partial u^B
  -V(u)
  +S_{\partial\mathrm{or\ defect}} .
\]
Dimension growth requires an infinite labelled family,
\[
  \lambda_n(\epsilon)^2
  =\lambda_0(\epsilon)^2
   +\frac{n^2}{R_{\mathrm{extra}}(\epsilon)^2}
   +\mathrm{residual}_n(\epsilon),
\]
with a source-computed gap scale.  Finite multiplet restoration gives
symmetry or phase restoration unless such a tower is present.

\section{Connes Anchors}

\subsection{Basic Spectral Data}

Source anchor.  A spectral triple is written as
\[
  (\mathcal A,\mathcal H,D).
\]
In even or real cases the additional data are
\[
  \gamma,\qquad J,
\]
with \(J\) carrying the right action and the \(\KO\)-signs.  In an
almost-commutative product the source convention used here is
\[
  \mathcal A=C^\infty(M)\otimes \AF,
  \qquad
  \mathcal H=L^2(M,S)\otimes \mathcal H_F,
\]
\[
  D=D_M\otimes 1+\gamma_M\otimes \DF.
\]
The inner fluctuation has the source form
\[
  \DA=D+A+\epsilon' JAJ^{-1},
  \qquad
  A=\sum_i a_i[D,b_i].
\]
The bosonic action is read through the spectral action
\[
  \Tr f(\DA/\Lambda).
\]

Source anchor.  The Standard Model finite algebra used as the NCG reference
is
\[
  \AF=\CC\oplus \HH\oplus M_3(\CC),
\]
with finite metric dimension zero and finite \(\KO\)-dimension six modulo
eight.  The distinction between metric dimension and \(\KO\)-dimension is
part of the translation: a finite algebra can carry \(\KO\)-signs without
becoming a smooth six-dimensional compact space.

\subsection{Source Dimension and Probe Dimension}

Conditional statement.  A charged sector can resolve only the compressed
algebra and operator available to that sector:
