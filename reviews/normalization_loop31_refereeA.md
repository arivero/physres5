# Loop 31 Referee A Report

Role: skeptical PRD referee focused on O10, electroweak correctness, scheme discipline, and overclaiming.

## Central Claim

O10 organizes the electroweak/Higgs DeVries construction as a dimensional interpolation
\[
(D,n_{\rm KK})=(11,7)\to(10,6)\to(9,5),
\]
with a colour-spectator version
\[
(D,n_{\rm KK}^{\rm ew})=(7,3)\to(6,2)\to(5,1).
\]
The middle lines are reconstruction labels.  They require a source operator,
two-channel light space, DeVries kernel, electroweak assignment, and pole map.
The proposal asks whether one source variable \(u\) controls geometry, the
electroweak ray, scalar data, the DeVries kernel, and a possible top-sector
datum.

\(SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)\) endpoint and its fermion quantum-number
obstruction.  The six-dimensional string references support a legitimate
six-dimensional string-vacuum arena.  The CHM audit supplies interval
boundary-operator ingredients: boundary kinetic terms, modified products,
vector Robin data, photon/custodial bookkeeping, and scalar boundary equations.

This supports Appendix E as theorem-target architecture.  It leaves the
physical interpolation as an open mechanism.

## Weakest Inference

The strongest unification assumption is that the same parameter \(u\) can
simultaneously control
\[
t_{\rm dim},\quad t_{\rm EW},\quad v,\quad m_h^2,\quad K_J,\quad
\mathcal F_{\rm sc},\quad \mathcal Y_{\rm top},
\]
and produce the \(D=10/D=6\) middle sector with the exact DeVries block.  The
source object, field space, and compactification family remain open.

A second weak point is endpoint language.  The allowed electroweak deformation
is the broken-to-unbroken radial ray with fixed \(g:g'\) and photon null
direction.  A heavy-\(W/Z\) decoupling endpoint is a separate geometric or
effective limit unless a source theorem derives compatibility with that ray.
