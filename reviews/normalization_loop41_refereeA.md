# Loop 41 Referee A Report

Role: skeptical PRD referee focused on correctness, scheme discipline, and
overclaiming.

## Central Claim

The manuscript presents the Rivero--de Vries quotient as a low-energy
complex-pole W/Z mass-ratio clue generated by a two-branch secular equation,
with endpoint, interval KK/CHM, superconnection, and \(G_2\) routes as source
mechanism candidates.

## Strongest Source-Supported Result

Scheme discipline is strong.  Sec. III separates complex poles,
Breit--Wigner conventions, \(\overline{\rm MS}\) angles, effective weak angles,
and high-scale boundary values.  Sec. IV derives the tree-level electroweak
projective ratio and the broken-to-unbroken vacuum ray.

## Weakest Inference

The ordered assignment
\[
P_W(h_J)=\frac34,\qquad P_Z(a_J^{\gamma^\perp})=2
\]
remains the main weak point.  The charged \(W\) is a gauge-sector pole, while
\(J=3/4\) belongs to the Higgs/order-parameter side.  Some equations still read
too much like exact matching statements.

- Photon-reference subtraction is gauge- and scheme-compatible.
- The CHM boundary kernel survives matching into a four-dimensional transverse
  complex pole.
- The superconnection grading supplies physical projectors after kinetic
  normalization.
- The negative eigenvector maps to a gauge-invariant scalar functional in the
  same basis.
