\section{Algebraic details}
\label{app:algebra}

For completeness, we record the elementary algebra.  From Eq.~\eqref{eq:charpoly},
\begin{equation}
  x_\pm(J)=\frac{-J\pm\sqrt{J(J+4)}}{2}.
\end{equation}
The positive branch has the large-\(J\) expansion
\begin{equation}
  x_+(J)=1-\frac{1}{J}+\frac{2}{J^2}+O(J^{-3}),
\end{equation}
while
\begin{equation}
  x_-(J)=-J-1+\frac{1}{J}+O(J^{-2}).
\end{equation}
Thus the positive branch approaches a fixed asymptotic value while the negative branch carries the large negative partner required by the trace.

For \(J=3/4\),
\begin{equation}
  x_+\left(\frac34\right)=\frac{-3/4+\sqrt{57}/4}{2}=\frac{\sqrt{57}-3}{8}.
\end{equation}
For \(J=2\),
\begin{equation}
  x_+(2)=\frac{-2+\sqrt{12}}{2}=\sqrt{3}-1.
\end{equation}
Therefore
\begin{equation}
  \sdV=1-\frac{\sqrt{57}-3}{8(\sqrt3-1)}.
\end{equation}
