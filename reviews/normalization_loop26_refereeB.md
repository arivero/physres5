The weak point is the passage from dimension count and source landscape to
DeVries interpolation sector.  The \(D=10/6\) middle line still needs a source
theory, field content, compact object, boundary condition, projection \(P_J\),
inner product, Schur complement, and anomaly-compatible light spectrum.

The \(D=9\) line also requires care: Witten's \(SU(3)\times U(1)\) geometric
address still needs the Standard Model electromagnetic charge lattice and
generator normalization.
