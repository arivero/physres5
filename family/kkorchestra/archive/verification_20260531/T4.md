## (b) Action of the fibre SO(3) on the candidate; the custodial pattern

The decisive structural facts (all checked, `calc/wb_T4c.py`, `calc/wb_T4d.py`):

1. **SO(3)_R acts on the FIBRE, not the doublet.** SU(2)_L-Casimir on m_0 (fibre) = 2 for all
   3 directions ⇒ **j=1 (triplet/adjoint)**; on m_3 (base) = 3/4 ⇒ **j=1/2 (two doublets)**.
   Ratio 2/(3/4)=8/3 confirmed. The Wilking SO(3)_R acts *transitively on m_0* (T2,
   Established) and j=1 there; the Higgs doublet is on m_3.

2. **No independent custodial SU(2) lives inside SU(3).** `dim centralizer(su(2)_L in su(3))
   = 1` (= u(1)_8 only). There is *no* second SU(2) commuting with SU(2)_L inside SU(3); a
   custodial SU(2)_R must come from the *external* Wilking SO(3) factor.

3. **The real (quaternionic) SO(4) structure on m_3 = C² = H is genuinely custodial.** Writing
   the 4 real horizontal scalars as quaternions H, left and right unit-quaternion
   multiplications give a commuting Sp(1)_L × Sp(1)_R = SO(4) acting as a **(2_L, 2_R)
   bidoublet** (rank of the 6 generators = 6, [L,R]=0, both verified). The right Cartan
   q→q·i = **diag(+i,−i)** (hypercharge-flipping relative to U(1)_8 = scalar diag(+i,+i)) and
   the right "swap" q→q·j is the antilinear map (z₁,z₂)→(−z₂*, z₁*) = **iσ₂K = exactly the SM
   custodial map H↔H̃** (matched up to sign in `wb_T4c.py`). So in *real/quaternionic* terms
   the 3-Sasakian Sp(1)_R **is** the operator that rotates H into H̃ — the defining custodial
   action that protects ρ.

4. **The custodial-breaking pattern is the correct one.** Gauging U(1)_Y (= a U(1) ⊂ SU(2)_L,
   the λ_8 line) does not commute with the *off-diagonal* Sp(1)_R generators, breaking
   Sp(1)_R → U(1)_{T3R}, precisely as in the SM where U(1)_Y breaks custodial SU(2)_R to
   U(1)_{T3R}. This is the SU(2)_L×SU(2)_R → SU(2)_diag custodial structure, with the diagonal
   surviving as the global symmetry that enforces ρ = m_W²/(m_Z²cos²θ_W) = 1 at tree level.

**However** (the obstruction, `wb_T4d.py`): the C-linear (holomorphic, gauge-compatible)
isometries of m_3 = C² are only **U(2) = SU(2)_L × U(1)_8 (dim 4)**. so(4) = 6 > 4: there is
**no room for a second independent holomorphic SU(2)_R** on the doublet. The two missing
generators so(4)/u(2) are precisely the **J-anti-holomorphic** part of Sp(1)_R — the
quaternionic-Kähler SU(2) that *rotates the complex structure triple* (I,J,K) by its SO(3)
adjoint (verified: conjugating I by exp(θ L_k) rotates I→cos2θ·I+sin2θ·J). This is the *same*
SO(3) that the Wilking factor realizes as **j=1 on the vertical fibre m_0**, *not* a
holomorphic doublet rotation on m_3. In geometric terms: SO(3)_R preserves the contact/Sasaki
3-plane and rotates the three complex structures; it does **not** preserve the single complex
structure J₀ = ad(H8)|_{m_3}/3 that defines "which doublet is the holomorphic Higgs." So the
custodial swap H↔H̃ is realized only anti-holomorphically and is **not** an isometry of the
gauge-fixed (complex) Higgs sector.

**Conclusion (b):** the fibre SO(3)_R reproduces the custodial SU(2)_R *structure* (right-
quaternion action, H↔H̃ swap, diag(+i,−i) Cartan, correct U(1)_Y breaking) but acts on the
*vertical fibre* as j=1, not as a holomorphic gauge symmetry of the *horizontal* Higgs
doublet. It is the right group with the right breaking pattern, in the wrong place unless one
adds dynamics that ties the fibre to the Higgs (see (d)).

## (c) SO(3)→SU(2) lift; j=1/2 (spin^c) vs j=1 (SO(3)_R) distribution

Exact parity law, verified for 3,8,10,15,27,6̄ (`wb_T4c.py`): **q8 ≡ 2I (mod 2)** for every
state in every SU(3) irrep (this is the triality/N-ality ↔ U(1)_8 link). Therefore:

- **Integer j ⇔ q8 even ⇔ honest SO(3)_R rep ⇔ scalar FUNCTIONS.** The fibre PU(2)=SO(3)
  (=SU(2)_L/Z₂, RP³) only carries single-valued (integer-spin) reps. The q8=0 towers give
  **j = 0,1,2,…** (8→j=1, 10→j=1, 27→j=2,1,0). The first harmonic is **j=1** = the (I,J,K)
  triplet = the genuine SO(3)_R rep. These need **no** spin^c line; X_{1,1} is spin (T1).

- **Half-integer j ⇔ q8 odd ⇔ double-valued on the fibre ⇔ spin^c-twisted (charged) sections.**
  j=1/2 (and 3/2,…) reps are NOT reps of SO(3); they are reps of its double cover SU(2)=Sp(1).
  They appear only as sections of the hypercharge line bundle L_n with **n odd** — precisely
  the T1 condition that odd n repairs the CP² spin (w₂) obstruction (minimal n=±1 = O(±1);
  canonical spin^c determinant n=±3 = O(±3)=K^{∓1}). **The Higgs doublet (j=1/2) sits at
  n=±3** (in the 8) and at n=±1 (in 3,3̄,6,6̄): it is a j=1/2 object living on the spin^c line.

So the C7-type split is geometrically natural and matches T1: the **j=1 / SO(3)_R** sector =
the **uncharged (q8=0) scalar functions on the vertical fibre** (no spin^c needed), while the
**j=1/2 / spin^c** sector = the **odd-hypercharge charged sections** that *require* the line
bundle L=O(odd) from T1 to be globally defined. This is the same mechanism Dolan–Nash use to
get one electroweak generation (with ν_R) from CP² spin^c twisted by O(n) line bundles (the
U(1) line-bundle charge = hypercharge; doublet vs singlet and their Y fixed by n) — here
realized on the 7D total space X_{1,1}, where the q8=0 fibre carries SO(3)_R and the q8=odd
charged sectors carry the spin^c half-integer reps.

**Fields requiring the spin^c line:** all SU(2)_L doublets/half-integer-j matter and Higgs
(q8 odd), i.e. exactly the would-be SM matter and Higgs, sit on L=O(odd) (T1). The SO(3)_R
gauge bosons and the j=integer scalar harmonics do not.

## (d) Extra input needed for the custodial identification to hold

The geometry (T1+T2 Established) supplies, *unconditionally*:
- an extra SO(3)_R isometry factor (Wilking), acting as j=1 on the vertical fibre m_0;
- the 3-Sasakian/QK SU(2) that, in the real quaternionic frame, has *exactly* the custodial
  algebra on m_3 = C² (right-quaternion (2_L,2_R) bidoublet; H↔H̃ swap; U(1)_Y→U(1)_{T3R}
  breaking);
- the spin^c line L=O(odd) on which the Higgs doublet (j=1/2, q8=±3) lives.

It does **NOT** supply, by geometry alone:

1. **(C3-physics)** A holomorphic gauge action of SO(3)_R on the *Higgs doublet*. SO(3)_R acts
   on the fibre (j=1), and its action on the base doublet is the *anti-holomorphic*
   complex-structure-rotating part of the QK SU(2), which is not a symmetry of the
   gauge-fixed complex Higgs. **Extra input required:** the Higgs scalar potential and Yukawa
   sector must be written in the *real (quaternionic) 3-Sasakian frame* so that Sp(1)_R is an
   **accidental global symmetry of the scalar potential** V(|Φ|²) — exactly as in the SM,
   where custodial SU(2) is an accidental symmetry of |Φ|² broken only by g′ and by
   up/down Yukawa splitting. Whether the KK-induced potential on X_{1,1} actually has this
   accidental Sp(1)_R requires constructing V from the reduction (not done; this is the open
   C3-physics conjecture). The *name* SO(3)_R = custodial SU(2)_R is therefore justified by
   the matching algebra/breaking pattern, but the *physical protection of ρ* is an assumption
   about the scalar dynamics, not a theorem of the geometry.

2. **(C5-caveat)** Because the isometry group SU(3)×SO(3) is **semisimple, not simple**, the
   SO(3)_R coupling relative to electroweak is a **free modulus** (relative size of the SU(3)
   vs SO(3) factor; the Wilking metric is only positively curved, the 3-Sasakian point is one
   special ratio t=+1 — T2). Custodial-protection *strength* is therefore **tunable, not
   predicted**: one can dial the SO(3)_R gauge coupling and the m_0/m_3 squashing
   (t) independently, so even granting the accidental symmetry, the size of custodial
   violations is not fixed by the construction. (This is T3(d)'s free-modulus statement.)

3. **Chirality / matter realization (cross-ref T6).** Identifying 2_{+3} as the *physical*
   Higgs (rather than just a harmonic) presupposes a chiral matter spectrum into which it
   couples; Witten's odd-dimension no-go (T6) is active for X_{1,1} and the spin^c twist does
   not evade it. So the "Higgs" here is a scalar harmonic with the right quantum numbers, not
   yet a Higgs of a chiral theory.

**Precise statement of the extra input:** *Beyond the geometry one must (i) posit that the
dimensionally-reduced Higgs potential is the SO(3)_R-symmetric quaternionic-frame potential
V(|Φ|²) so that Sp(1)_R is an accidental symmetry breaking as SU(2)_L×SU(2)_R→SU(2)_diag, and
(ii) fix the SU(3):SO(3) relative coupling/squashing modulus (semisimple, hence free) to make
the custodial coupling phenomenologically relevant.* Under (i)+(ii) the Wilking SO(3) **is**
the physical custodial SU(2)_R; without them it is a geometric SO(3) with the correct algebra
and breaking pattern but no enforced ρ-protection — i.e. **more than a name coincidence, less
than a derivation.**
