# Closure Progress Audit, Loops 1--48

## Audit Question

This report answers whether the previous loops were working on closure plans for
the pending analytical issues.

Closure means one of two outcomes:

- a source-backed derivation enters the manuscript;
- a route receives a precise rejection result with stated consequences.

Closure progress means a loop moves an issue toward that standard by naming the
source package, basis, projection, inner product, scale, acceptance equation,
rejection output, or next derivation step.
