rendering the theory inconsistent. However, no such restriction exists for “large” transformations,
that can be broken without disastrous consequences.

anomalous symmetries are crucial in our understanding of a number of physical phenomena, it is about
time to look into anomalies in some detail (see [93–96] for some reviews on the topic).
