# M-Theory-Style Tower Test For Extra Dimension

## Point

The analogy with Witten's IIA-to-M-theory jump requires a candidate extra
direction carrying an infinite labelled family of states, modes, defects, or
boundary characters whose spectrum reorganizes as a Kaluza-Klein tower in the
limiting regime.

A finite restoration of a carrier block, such as a four-real-scalar `m_3`
Higgs multiplet around `<H>=0`, supports symmetry restoration. By itself it
does not support an emergent extra dimension in the M-theory sense.

## Benchmark Mechanism

Witten's `hep-th/9503124` source gives the benchmark in a form useful for
the present project. In the Type IIA strong-coupling limit, the candidate
new circle has radius

```text
r(lambda) = lambda^(2/3)
```

in the eleven-dimensional metric, while the charge-`n` tower has masses of
order

```text
M_n(lambda) ~ |n|/lambda
```

in the ten-dimensional string metric.

The benchmark has three ingredients.

1. A modulus controls the radius of the emergent circle:
   `R_11` grows with the IIA coupling.
2. An infinite charge family exists before the geometric reinterpretation:
   D0 charge `n`.
3. The family has masses compatible with KK momentum on the new circle:
   `M_n ~ n/R_11`.

The evidential object is the infinite family plus its scaling law. The slogan
"an extra dimension appears" follows after that calculation.

The second useful source lesson comes from Witten's `hep-th/9512219`.
Orbifolds and fixed-point/five-brane charge assignments can repair a
classical geometric obstruction through localized sources and anomaly
bookkeeping. For our project, the analogy points toward branch-locus,
boundary, or defect labels on the `CP^2 -> S^4` cover, rather than toward a
smooth homogeneous space alone.

## Required Form In The KK-Higgs Program

For the proposed `D6/D2 -> D7/D3` growth mechanism, use a parameter
`epsilon` or `v` and compute an infinite family

```text
F_n(epsilon),     n in Z or n in N,
```

with a limiting spectrum of the form

```text
M_n(epsilon)^2 = M_0(epsilon)^2 + n^2/R_extra(epsilon)^2 + lower-order terms
```

or a signed/action analogue with the same large-label organization.

Positive evidence requires:

1. `R_extra(epsilon)` increases, or the relevant gap decreases, in the
   proposed growth limit.
2. The labels `n` have a geometric or topological meaning tied to the extra
   direction: Fourier mode, monopole sector, flat character, covering-sheet
   winding, defect charge, eta-family label, or boundary spectral label.
3. The source action computes the scaling. It cannot be inserted as a desired
   outcome.
4. The broken finite branch still passes the custodial `rho=1` condition when
   the branch has `<Phi_0>=0` and isotropic `m_3`.

Failure mode:

```text
finite m_3 restoration without a tower
```

Then the result remains a Higgs/symmetry-restoration mechanism, not an
M-theory-style emergent-dimension mechanism.

## Candidate Infinite Families

The current project has several plausible places to look for the required
label.

1. **Boundary Fourier tower.** A collar or circle coordinate in the D5/D1
   endpoint gives modes `n`. The limit needs to show that these modes become
   the missing D6/D2 or D7/D3 direction.
2. **Flat-character tower before quotienting.** The finite D5 lens-space
   character set might be a residue of a larger `Z` family. The calculation
   needs to explain how finite characters approximate, truncate, or descend
   from an infinite spectral family.
3. **Covering-sheet or branch-defect winding.** The `CP^2 -> S^4` branched
   cover can carry defect data around the branch locus. An infinite winding or
   boundary spectral family would make the cover do more than provide a
   finite geometric comparison.
4. **Dirac or DtN boundary spectrum.** Calderon, eta, or Dirichlet-to-Neumann
   data naturally provide infinite eigenvalue labels. A useful branch needs a
   controlled limit where the label behaves like KK momentum.
5. **Representation ladder.** A representation label can form a tower when
   the action computes a physical gap and a radius-like parameter. Bare
   representation notation by itself provides bookkeeping, not a dimension.
6. **Localized branch-locus source family.** Witten's orbifold paper suggests
   looking for localized source labels on a singular or branched set. The
   useful output would be a spectral family tied to defect charge, not a
   finite list of exceptional points.

## Counterargument

The analogy can fail in three clean ways.

1. The limit restores a finite number of scalar components.
2. An infinite spectral label exists, but its gap stays finite or increases.
3. The tower appears after imposing the desired spectrum by hand.

Any of these outcomes deletes the M-theory-style interpretation from the
active branch. The remaining result can still be valuable as a custodial
Higgsing or boundary-phase mechanism.

## Constructive Route

A narrow calculation can test the idea without importing De Vries premises.

1. Choose the candidate family: collar Fourier modes, boundary DtN spectrum,
   branch-defect winding, or flat-character lift.
2. Write the reduced quadratic action with the parameter `epsilon` or `v`.
3. Diagonalize the family and record `M_n(epsilon)`.
4. Check whether the asymptotics have a common radius scale
   `R_extra(epsilon)`.
5. If the gap closes in the growth limit, keep the branch as an
   extra-dimension candidate.
6. If the gap does not close, retain any bosonic constraints and delete the
   emergent-dimension interpretation.

## Status

The project now has a sharper distinction:

```text
finite restored multiplet        -> symmetry restoration
infinite tower with closing gap   -> extra-dimension candidate
```

The next concrete search should target the boundary/covering data, because
the `m_3` Higgs block alone has four real scalar components.
