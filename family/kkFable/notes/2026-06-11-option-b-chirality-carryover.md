Date: 2026-06-11.

## Expansion block

- Domain/operator: the (possibly twisted) Dirac operator on the 7-dimensional
  X_{1,1}; 4D fermions = its KK modes in the 11D-type split M⁴ × X⁷.
- Mechanism (T6, Established; cited): in 7 dimensions the Clifford algebra
  Cl(7) is simple and admits a charge-conjugation pairing under which every
  internal eigenmode appears with its conjugate; the induced 4D spectrum
  pairs left- and right-handed modes with identical gauge quantum numbers —
  vector-like. The spin^c line (T1's O(k), k odd) changes which bundles
  exist, not the pairing; T6 verified explicitly that spin^c does not evade
  the no-go. Witten's original framing: NPB 186 (1981) 412 and the 1983
  Shelter Island odd-dimension argument.
- Identification-independence: no step references which SU(3) is colour;
  the conclusion is a property of (dim X = 7, spinor algebra, real structure)
  fixed by C1–C4.
- Status: cited (T6, two literature anchors), with the independence argument
  derived here in one line.

## Counterargument pass

*"Coupling the Dolan–Nash base index (N6 §6, nonzero for n odd ≥ 3) to 4D
chirality: does a nonzero index on CP² not give chiral 4D modes?"* The base
index counts chiral zero modes of the 4D-base-auxiliary problem; assembled
on the full odd-dimensional X the conjugate tower re-pairs them (T6's
explicit check). The index survives as a multiplicity/organization statement
(N6 §6), not as 4D chirality. No change.
