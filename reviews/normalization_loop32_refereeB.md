# Loop 32 Referee B

## Central Claim

O3 claims that \(x_-(J)\), after the positive branch is normalized by the W/Z
vector spectrum, may encode scalar or order-parameter data: Higgs-potential
curvature, radial vacuum scale, boundary modulus, brane separation mode,
Wilson-line scalar, or \(G_2\) deformation.  The manuscript frames this as a
theorem target.

## Strongest Supported Result

The strongest supported result is structural.  Sec. V and Appendix D Target IV
require a gauge-invariant scalar functional
\[
\mathcal F_{\rm sc}=C\,|x_-(J_\star)|
\]
with specified \(J_\star\), normalization, and scheme.  The source ledgers
support candidate scalar variables: Higgs radial data from Logan and Dawson,
interval boundary/radion/Wilson-line data from CHM, Bucci, Haba-Oda, and
Hosotani, brane scalar language from Tong and Polchinski, and \(G_2\)
localization/deformation language from Acharya-Witten, Acharya-Gukov, and Braun
et al.  BF supplies a caveat for negative mass-squared scalar readings.

## Weakest Inference

The scalar-scale statement lacks a specified observable, scheme, and source
operator.  The route maps are plausible source addresses.  The derived scalar
theorem remains open.

## Hidden Assumptions

1. The positive and negative branches survive matching from the same physical
   two-channel operator.
2. The negative eigenvector has overlap with a physical scalar channel.
3. The vector normalization \(M_Z^2=\mu^2x_+(2)\) also fixes the scalar
   normalization constant.
4. The selected \(J_\star\) is fixed by source data.
5. Extra light channels in endpoint, interval, or \(G_2\) reductions can be
   projected out while preserving the determinant.
6. Potential parameters, pole masses, radion masses, Wilson-line moduli, and
   \(G_2\) deformation eigenvalues can be compared after scheme and matching
   maps are supplied.

## Required Revisions

1. In Sec. V, convert scalar-scale language into a conjectural ledger:
   candidate observable, formula, scheme, source route, and missing derivation.
2. Add a compact table separating source-supported scalar variables from the
   missing DeVries map for endpoint, interval, and \(G_2\).
3. In Appendix D Target IV, add acceptance subconditions: same
   \(\mathcal H_J\), same inner product, same projection \(P_J\), same
   normalization, and an auxiliary-branch failure outcome.
4. In O3, require the scalar functional to come from the same source reduction
   used for Targets I--III.
5. In the concept matrix, revise the negative-branch row around source
   addresses for scalar variables and the open scalar map.
6. In the source inventory, mark the \(G_2\), endpoint, and interval scalar
   entries as route ingredients with rendered-PDF formula checks pending.

## Score

O3 readiness: 3/5 as an open theorem target.
